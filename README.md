## What it is

From the official overview page:

> UGREEN is opening AI NAS to developers through UGOS and Skills Hub.
>
> Powered by UGOS, UGREEN's own Linux-based operating system, UGREEN AI NAS brings local storage, computing, and AI capabilities together in one platform. [...]
>
> With the UGOS Skill Creation Tool and your preferred AI assistant, you can create custom Skills that use supported NAS capabilities to organize files, find information, and automate everyday tasks.

> **What to Build:** Build an AI Skill that runs on UGREEN AI NAS. Use the UGREEN Skill Developer Package and its technical docs to build it. Choose one track that best fits your Skill.

- **Format:** Online, Public. Devpost lists the themes as Lifehacks, Machine Learning/AI, and Productivity.
- **No UGREEN NAS is required to enter.** "Finalists will receive free access to a UGREEN AI NAS iDX6011 Pro environment for final development, testing, and validation."
- **Two rounds:**
  - **Round 1 (open to all):** "Share your idea and an early build of your Skill. [...] submit a short PDF and a Pre-test Skill Package (ZIP)."
  - **Round 2 (Finalists only):** "UGREEN will select up to 30 Finalists. Each Finalist gets one assigned testing day of free remote access to a real UGREEN AI NAS [...] Finalists then submit their final Skill and a short demo video".

### Tracks (overview page wording)

1. **Automate Your Workflow:** "Build an AI Skill that uses authorized NAS files and data, together with the file, document, knowledge, and system capabilities provided for the Hackathon, to organize content, extract information, generate summaries, and streamline repetitive tasks."
2. **Bring Memories to Life:** "Build an AI Skill that works with authorized photos, videos, and documents stored on UGREEN AI NAS to organize or search content, extract text, generate summaries, or create albums."
3. **Unleash Your Creativity:** "Build an AI Skill that helps photographers, editors, designers, and other content creators search, tag, select, organize, and review media stored on UGREEN AI NAS, making creative preparation faster and more efficient."
4. **Open Innovation:** "Go beyond the tracks above by combining the NAS, AI, media, file, and knowledge capabilities provided for the challenge to build an original AI Skill that addresses a real user need and runs on UGREEN AI NAS."

FAQ: "Pick the one track that best fits your Skill. Tracks help judges understand your idea. All prizes are open to every track."
