## Discrepancies between official pages

The Official Rules state: "If there is any discrepancy or inconsistency between the terms and conditions of the Official Rules and disclosures or other statements contained in any Hackathon materials, [...] the terms and conditions of the Official Rules shall prevail." (§11)

- **Submissions open.** The Devpost Schedule page says "October 22 at 9:15am PDT" (Sydney: Fri 23 Oct, 03:15). The Rules say 9:00 am PT.
- **Judging.** The Schedule page shows a single "Judging" period from "November 16 at 12:00am PST" to "December 05 at 5:00pm PST". It does not split Round 1 and Round 2.
- **Winners Announced.** The Schedule page says "December 06 at 2:00am PST" (Sydney: Sun 6 Dec, 21:00). The Rules say "2:00 pm Pacific Time" (Sydney: Mon 7 Dec, 09:00).
- **Overview and FAQ shorthand.** These pages say "by November 15" (Round 1) and "by November 30" (Round 2). They agree with the Rules.
