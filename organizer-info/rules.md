# Rules (selected sections)

The full text is in the Official Rules: https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules. "SUBMISSION OF ANY ENTRY CONSTITUTES AGREEMENT TO THESE OFFICIAL RULES". If any Hackathon materials conflict with the Official Rules, "the terms and conditions of the Official Rules shall prevail" (§11). "NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN."

## Eligibility (§3)

*Source: https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules §3*

**Open to:**
- "Individuals who are at least the age of majority where they reside as of the time of entry"
- "Teams of up to three Eligible Individuals"

**Allowed countries and territories, Asia-Pacific:** "Taiwan, Hong Kong SAR, Macao SAR, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Singapore, South Korea, Japan, **Australia**, New Zealand, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Philippines, Cambodia, Pakistan, Maldives, Laos, and Mongolia." The other regions are listed in full in §3. They cover named countries in the Americas (Canada excluding Quebec), Europe, the Middle East and Caucasus, and Africa.

**Not open to (summary of §3):**
- Residents of excluded countries or territories. The list includes Quebec, **China**, India, Brazil, Russia, Vietnam, and many others; see §3 for the full list.
- **Organizations**: "(including corporations, not-for-profit corporations and other nonprofit organizations, limited liability companies, partnerships, and other legal entities)". The overview page also says "Companies/professional organizations excluded from participation".
- Promotion Entities (including the Sponsor and Administrator), their employees, representatives and agents, and their immediate family or household members.
- Judges, and any company or individual that employs a Judge.
- Anyone whose participation would create "a real or apparent conflict of interest".

Note: the Devpost "Who can participate" excluded-country list on the overview page does not include China. The Rules list it and prevail.

## Teams (§3, §4, FAQ)

*Sources: https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules §3–§4; https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/resources (FAQ)*

- "Teams of up to three Eligible Individuals"
- "Each Eligible Individual may enter either individually or as a member of one Team, but not both. An Eligible Individual may not join more than one Team."
- A team "must appoint and authorize one individual (the 'Representative') to represent, act, and enter a Submission, on their behalf. The Representative must meet the eligibility requirements above."
- Finalist NAS credentials: "For Teams and Organizations, UGREEN sends the credentials only to the Representative."
- Prizes for a team are paid to the Representative, who allocates them "among their Team [...] as the Representative deems appropriate" (§8).
- "Each individual Entrant or Team may submit only one Project during the Hackathon. The Final Round submission must be the final version of the same Project submitted in Round 1 and does not count as an additional Project." (§4, Multiple Submissions)
- "a person outside the team may not develop the Project or create its submission materials for the Entrant." (§4, Intellectual Property)

## Project requirements (§4)

*Source: https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules §4*

- "Create an AI Skill for UGREEN AI NAS using the official UGOS Skill Creation Tool and the development resources provided in the Hackathon Starter Kit."
- "Skills must follow the official Uliya Skill Development Standard and use supported UGOS CLI capabilities through registered expert agents. Custom executable scripts, general-purpose programming runtimes, and unauthorized direct access to NAS resources are not supported."
- "Entrants are encouraged to use the AI NAS local AI model, but its use is not required. Properly authorized third-party models may also be used".
- **New and existing projects:** "An Entrant may build on an idea conceived before the Hackathon, but the submitted Skill and its core workflow must be newly developed during the Hackathon. Previously developed Skills or submission materials may not be reused. [...] Round 2 Submissions must build on the same Project submitted in Round 1."
- **Third-party integrations:** "An Entrant may use third-party SDKs, APIs, components, and data only when it has the right to do so and complies with the applicable terms and licenses. The Proposal must identify third-party components, their sources and applicable licenses, and any external services and data flows."
- **Financial or preferential support:** a Project "must not have been developed, or derived from a Project developed, with financial or preferential support from the Sponsor or Administrator."
- **Use of tools:** "Use of the developer tools, the UGREEN Developer Skill Package, and any UGREEN AI NAS access is subject to the license agreement related thereto." The Starter Kit also requires agreement to the SkillHub User Agreement, Privacy Policy, and Skill Content Safety and Publishing Guidelines (see resources.md).
- **Finalist NAS use:** "Finalists may use the assigned NAS only to install, test, and record their Skill for this Hackathon." Finalists must back up any data they want to keep, such as runtime logs and test data, before the testing period ends: "Data deleted during the reset process cannot be recovered."
- Round 2: "The Entrant must keep the Project available free of charge, without restriction, for testing and evaluation by the Sponsor and Judges until the Round 2 Judging Period ends."

## Data and external services (FAQ)

*Source: https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/resources (FAQ "Can my Skill send data to outside services?")*

> Your Skill may transmit data to cloud AI services supported by Uliya when necessary for AI processing, subject to platform permissions and data security requirements. You should use synthetic or appropriately anonymized test data.
>
> Do not send real personal, private or confidential information, sensitive NAS files, account credentials or API keys to external services. Unauthorized external transfers, direct calls to unsupported third-party APIs and attempts to bypass platform security controls are prohibited.

## AI tools (FAQ)

*Source: https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/resources (FAQ "Can I use AI tools?")*

> Yes. You can use AI coding assistants and other standard development tools. You must list every AI tool you used, and how you used it, in your disclosures.

The FAQ also gives this advice (recommendation only): "Our advice: be human where it counts. [...] Where we recommend you skip AI: Naming your project; Writing your text description and PDF answers; Coming up with your core idea." Great uses for AI, per the FAQ: help writing and debugging code, speeding up repetitive tasks, spelling and grammar, and subtitles or voiceover for the demo video.

## Submission modifications (§5)

*Source: https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules §5*

"Before the end of each Submission Period, you may save draft versions of your Submission. Once a Submission Period ends, you may not change the Submission for that round, but you may continue to update the Project in your Devpost portfolio. Do not delete your Round 1 Submission materials." Limited changes after a deadline are possible only to remove infringing, PII, or inappropriate material, with Sponsor and Devpost permission.

## Judging (§6)

*Source: https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules §6*

- The process "may utilize expert panels, peer review, automated AI-driven analysis, or any combination thereof [...] Final eligibility and judging decisions are made by humans."
- **Round 1:** "The first stage will determine via pass/fail whether the ideas meet a baseline level of viability, in that the Project reasonably fits the theme and reasonably proposes an AI NAS solution. Eligible Projects will then be scored according to the Judging Criteria [...] based on the Round 1 Submission materials, including the PDF and ZIP Pre-test Skill Package [...] A lack of NAS runtime evidence will not, by itself, make a Submission ineligible or lower its Round 1 score. Up to thirty (30) of the highest-scoring eligible Projects will become Finalists."
- **Round 2:** Judges evaluate "their working Skill in the assigned UGREEN AI NAS environment, the video, and the documentation, including whether the ideas in the Round 1 Submission were delivered. **Round 1 scores do not carry over. Winners are decided by Round 2 scores only.**"
- If a Finalist cannot proceed, "the Sponsor may invite the next highest-scoring eligible Project in its place."

**Judging criteria (Rounds 1 and 2), equally weighted:**

| Criterion | Official Rules wording | Overview page wording |
|---|---|---|
| User Value | "Whether the Skill addresses a clear user problem and gives users a clear workflow, useful feedback, and a meaningful result." | "Does the Skill solve a clear user problem? Does it give users a clear workflow, useful feedback, and a real result?" |
| Innovation & Differentiation | "Whether the Skill offers a substantive new function, interaction, or workflow that differs from conventional AI tools and existing solutions." | "Does the Skill offer a new function, interaction, or workflow that is different from common AI tools and existing solutions?" |
| Technical Quality | "Whether the Skill demonstrates a sound orchestration approach, appropriate use of supported CLI capabilities and expert agents, proper handling of permissions and errors, and reliable execution appropriate to its development stage." | "Do the core and repeat-use cases work? Does the Skill handle errors and edge cases? Does it manage data, permissions, credentials, outside services, and versions well? Do the docs and dependencies support install, setup, removal, and testing?" |
| AI NAS Fit & Validation | "Whether the Skill makes meaningful use of UGREEN AI NAS capabilities, including relevant local data, storage, computing, privacy, or other supported capabilities, rather than merely running on NAS." | "Do UGREEN AI NAS features add real value to the Skill? Does the Skill use NAS resources well?" |

**Tie-breaking:** ties are broken by the score on the first listed criterion, then the next, and so on, with a judges' vote as the last resort. For Most Creative, ties on Innovation & Differentiation are broken by the highest overall score, then by a judges' vote.

Judges: "A panel of qualified judges" (overview). Individual judges are Not stated.

## Intellectual property and licensing (§4, §7)

*Source: https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules §4 and §7*

- "All Submissions remain the intellectual property of the individuals or organizations that developed them. By submitting an entry, entrants agree that the Sponsor will have a non-exclusive license to use such entry for judging the entry."
- "Entrants agree that the sponsor and Devpost shall have the right to promote the Submission and use the name, likeness, voice and image of all individuals contributing to a Submission, in any materials promoting or publicizing the Hackathon and its results, during the Hackathon Period and for three years thereafter."
- The Submission must "(a) be your (or your Team, or Organization's) original work product; (b) be solely owned by you [...]; and (c) not violate the intellectual property rights or other rights".
- Open source: an Entrant may use open-source software "provided the Entrant complies with applicable open source licenses and, as part of the Submission, creates software that enhances and builds upon the features and functionality included in the underlying open source product."
- "Properly licensed third-party components and general technical assistance through the UGREEN Community are permitted".
- Publicity (§10): the Entrant consents to promotion of the Submission and to use of their name, likeness, voice, and similar details "worldwide without further payment".
- Required open-source license for submissions: Not stated.

## Prizes: conditions (§8)

*Source: https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/rules §8*

- Each Project can win only one Prize. Prizes are non-transferable.
- Winners must complete affidavits and Required Forms within "ten (10) business days after the Required Forms are sent". Prizes are delivered "within 60 days" of receipt of the completed forms.
- Winners pay any fees and taxes. Non-US residents "may be required to provide a completed W-8BEN form". Withholding may apply.
