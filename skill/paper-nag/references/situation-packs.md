# Situation packs

Use a pack when the user describes a situation (for example "my mother is going into hospital" or "we are moving next month"). Show the pack items, let the user confirm, then check them with the same Steps 2–6 as the main checklist. A pack replaces the main checklist for that run unless the user asks for both.

These packs are general. Requirements differ by country, insurer and institution; the user confirms what applies.

## Contents

- Hospital stay or health insurance claim
- Moving house
- New baby
- Travelling abroad
- Vehicle accident or claim
- Loss of a family member

## Hospital stay or health insurance claim

Health insurance policy or card; identity document of the patient; referral letter; hospital admission or discharge summary; itemized hospital invoices and receipts; medical report or diagnosis; pharmacy receipts; claim form, if the insurer uses one; bank details for reimbursement, recorded without full account numbers.

## Moving house

New lease or sale contract; move-in condition report and dated condition photos; bond or deposit receipt; previous lease and move-out photos; home or contents insurance; proof of address; vehicle registration; school or childcare enrolment records.

## New baby

Birth certificate or birth registration; parents' identity documents; hospital discharge summary; vaccination record; proof that the child was added to health insurance; updated will and guardianship nomination.

## Travelling abroad

Passport for every traveller (at least 6 months of validity); visas or travel authorizations; travel insurance policy; vaccination certificates where required; driver licence; emergency contact list; booking confirmations.

## Vehicle accident or claim

Vehicle insurance policy; vehicle registration; driver licence; **photos of the damage taken on the day** (search photos by date); repair quotes and invoices; police or incident report number, if one was made.

## Loss of a family member

Handle this pack gently and only when the user asks for it. Death certificate; will; life insurance policies; pension or retirement statements and beneficiary nominations; property deed or lease; recent account statements, recorded without full account numbers; funeral contract or invoices; identity document of the executor.
