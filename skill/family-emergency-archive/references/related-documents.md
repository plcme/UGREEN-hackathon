# Related-document rules

Use these rules in Step 5 of the Skill. When a document or fact in the left column is Found (or the user confirms it), add the documents in the right column to the checklist for the same member or household, if they are not already on it. In the report, show each added item with its reason, for example "Vehicle insurance (because a vehicle registration was found)".

## Contents

## Linked groups (any one implies the others)

If any document in a group is found, every other document in the same group is expected for the same vehicle, property or person.

| Group | Documents |
|---|---|
| Vehicle | Vehicle purchase contract or invoice; vehicle registration; vehicle insurance; vehicle warranty (while under warranty); driver licence of the main driver |
| Owned home | Property deed or purchase contract; purchase invoice or tax receipt; mortgage contract (if financed); home or property insurance; property management or strata agreement (if applicable); electricity, water, gas and internet accounts |
| Rented home | Lease agreement; bond or deposit receipt; move-in condition report; dated condition photos (see below); electricity, water, gas and internet accounts |
| Employment | Employment contract; social insurance contribution history; CV or resume; graduation or degree certificate |

## One-way rules

| If this is found or confirmed | Then also expect | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| User is a landlord (rental lease where the user is the lessor) | Landlord insurance; bond or deposit record; condition report and dated condition photos for each tenancy | per rental property |
| Any child in the household (a member under 18 by the birth year found, or named as a child by the user) | Birth certificate; vaccination record; allergy record; guardianship nomination | each child; household |
| Life insurance policy | Beneficiary nomination; will | the policy holder |
| Pension or retirement account | Beneficiary nomination | the account holder |
| Loan or mortgage contract | Latest loan statement | household |
| Invoice for a large appliance or electronics | Warranty card while under warranty; repair receipts if repaired | household |
| Will | Executor name in the emergency contact list | household |
| Regular medication in a medical report | Current medication list; allergy record | that member |
| Recent trips abroad (confirmed by the user) | Passport validity; visas; travel insurance | travellers |

## Job that needs a degree or licence

Use the job title extracted from the employment contract or CV in Step 3. If the job normally requires a degree or a professional licence, mark the degree certificate, academic transcript and professional licence for that member as **important**, and list them first among that member's Missing items with the reason, for example "Degree certificate (important: job title Registered Nurse usually requires it)".

Jobs that usually need them include: doctor, nurse, pharmacist, dentist, lawyer, accountant, auditor, engineer, architect, teacher, lecturer, psychologist, social worker, and licensed trades such as electrician or plumber. If unsure, ask the user instead of guessing.

## Close to retirement

Do not ask the user for ages. Use the birth year found in a member's identity documents in Step 3; if none was found, skip this rule for that member. If the member is within 5 years of the usual retirement age in the confirmed country of residence (use 60 when that age is not known, and say so in the report), add these items for that member: pension or retirement account statements; social insurance contribution history; proof of employment history; identity documents; bank account details for pension payments (no full account numbers). Show the reason, for example "Retirement documents (because Alex will reach retirement age in 3 years)".

## Condition photos check

Condition photos are the evidence a tenant or landlord needs if there is a dispute when a tenancy ends. For every lease found:

1. Take the lease start date (and end date, if the tenancy has ended) from the extracted lease fields.
2. Ask `photo-agent` for photos in the confirmed folders taken from 14 days before to 14 days after each date.
3. If photos exist, mark "Condition photos" as Found and give the folder or album and the photo count. If none exist, mark it Missing and explain why it matters in one line.

The Skill only checks that dated photos exist. It does not judge the condition of the property.
