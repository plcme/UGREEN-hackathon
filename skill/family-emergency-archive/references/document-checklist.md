# Default emergency document checklist

Use this list in Step 1 of the Skill. Show the category names to the user, let them remove items that do not apply, and add their own items. Items marked **each member** are checked for every household member; **each adult** only for adults; **household** once for the whole family. Related documents that one document implies are in [related-documents.md](related-documents.md).

The structure follows the sections of the public Emergency Financial First Aid Kit (identification, financial and legal documents, medical information, contacts), extended and made country-neutral. Requirements and names differ by country; the user decides what applies.

## Contents

- Identity and family status
- Education and work
- Social insurance and retirement
- Banking, loans and tax
- Insurance
- Home and utilities
- Vehicles
- Major purchases and contracts
- Legal
- Medical
- Emergency contacts
- Keywords in other languages
