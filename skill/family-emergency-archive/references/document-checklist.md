# Default emergency document checklist

Use this list in Step 1 of the Skill. Show the category names to the user, let them remove items that do not apply, and add their own items. Items marked **each member** are checked for every household member; **each adult** only for adults; **household** once for the whole family.

The structure follows the four sections of the public Emergency Financial First Aid Kit (identification, financial and legal documents, medical information, contacts), adapted to be country-neutral. Requirements differ by country; the user decides what applies.

## Contents

- Identity
- Insurance
- Property and finance
- Legal
- Medical
- Emergency contacts
- Keywords in other languages

## Identity

| Item | Applies to | Has expiry | Search keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passport | each member | yes (6-month window) | passport |
| National identity card | each member | yes | identity card, ID card |
| Driver licence | each adult | yes | driver licence, driving licence, driver license |
| Birth certificate | each member | no | birth certificate |
| Residence permit or visa | each member (if applicable) | yes | residence permit, visa |
| Marriage or civil partnership certificate | household | no | marriage certificate |

## Insurance

| Item | Applies to | Has expiry | Search keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| Health insurance policy or card | each member | renewal date | health insurance, medical insurance |
| Life insurance policy | each adult | renewal date | life insurance, policy number |
| Home or renters insurance | household | renewal date | home insurance, renters insurance, contents insurance |
| Vehicle insurance | household (if a vehicle is owned) | renewal date | car insurance, motor insurance, vehicle insurance |

## Property and finance

| Item | Applies to | Has expiry | Search keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| Property deed or lease agreement | household | lease end date | deed, title, lease agreement, tenancy agreement |
| Vehicle registration | household (if a vehicle is owned) | yes | vehicle registration |
| List of bank and pension accounts (no full numbers) | each adult | no | bank account, pension, superannuation, retirement |
| Most recent tax return | each adult | no | tax return, tax assessment |
| Loan or mortgage statement | household (if applicable) | no | mortgage, loan statement |

## Legal

| Item | Applies to | Has expiry | Search keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| Will | each adult | no | will, last will, testament |
| Power of attorney | each adult | no | power of attorney |
| Advance healthcare directive | each adult | no | advance directive, healthcare directive, living will |
| Guardianship nomination for children | household (if children) | no | guardian, guardianship |

## Medical

| Item | Applies to | Has expiry | Search keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vaccination record | each member | no | vaccination, immunisation, immunization |
| Most recent medical report or check-up | each member | no | medical report, health check, check-up |
| Current medication and allergy list | each member | no | medication, prescription, allergy |

Medical documents are listed only. The Skill never interprets medical results.

## Emergency contacts

| Item | Applies to | Has expiry | Search keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emergency contact list (family, doctor, insurer, employer) | household | no | emergency contacts, contact list |

## Keywords in other languages

If the user writes in another language, add the equivalent keywords in that language for the OCR and semantic searches (for example Chinese: 护照, 身份证, 驾照, 出生证明, 结婚证, 保险单, 房产证, 租赁合同, 遗嘱, 授权委托书, 疫苗接种, 体检报告).
